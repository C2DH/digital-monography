De Gruyter academic publishing

As an independent academic publisher, we publish over 1,500 books and 16,000 journal articles every year, across 30 subject areas. This includes more than 200 books and 6,000 articles in open access. We also offer some 80 databases and online reference works, covering an extremely broad spectrum of disciplines.
